Most people learn Openslot by clicking around, and that mostly works. This course is for the parts that do not come up until later: the availability rule that would have prevented a bad week, the reminder that stops a no-show, the team page that gives a coordinator their morning back.

Six lessons, 1 hour 10 minutes in total. Each one is a short video with a written version underneath, so you can read it instead when that is faster, and each ends with one thing to set up in your own workspace before the next lesson.

It is free, nothing is gated, and all of it can be done in the account you already have. The average time from signup to first booking is 2 days, and the first lesson covers that part on its own.

## The lessons

- Your first booking page, 10 min. Event types, durations, what belongs in the description, and the link you send to people.
- Availability rules that hold up, 14 min. Working hours per weekday, buffers before and after, daily limits, minimum notice and date ranges, with the numbers most teams settle on.
- Reminders, reschedules and cancellations, 11 min. Email reminders on every plan, SMS on paid plans, and why every message should carry a reschedule link.
- Team pages: round-robin and collective, 13 min. When a booking should rotate across a team and when every invited teammate has to be free. Both are on the Team plan.
- Embedding a booking page, 9 min. Inline and popup embeds in two lines of markup, where to put them on a page, and how they behave on a phone.
- Workflows and webhooks, 13 min. Running something after a booking is made, moved or cancelled, and sending the same event on to your own systems.

## Who it's for

- New workspaces in their first week, before any habits have had time to form.
- Operations people setting Openslot up for a team rather than only for themselves.
- Anyone who has used a booking link for a year and never opened the settings behind it.
