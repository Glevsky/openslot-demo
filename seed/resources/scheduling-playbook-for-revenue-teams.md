A prospect is at their most interested in the ten minutes after they ask for a call. Most revenue teams spend the next two days proving otherwise, in an email chain about whether Tuesday works. By the time a slot is agreed, the reason for the call has cooled. The request is the warmest part of the whole process, and the calendar is what cools it.

This playbook is the setup we run ourselves and the one we see working on teams of two reps and teams of sixty. It is organized by the problem you are fixing rather than by feature, so you can open it at the stage that hurts and leave the rest alone.

Forty-eight pages, with every setting written out in full. Where a choice depends on how your team is built, the page gives both options and the conditions each one suits, instead of pretending there is one right answer. Two worked examples run the length of the book, one for a single rep handling inbound requests and one for an eight-person team splitting them.

## What's inside

- Routing forms: one question before the calendar, so a request reaches the right rep the first time.
- Round-robin pages with ownership rules, so an existing account still lands with the person who owns it.
- Collective pages for the demo that needs a solutions engineer in the room, and how to keep them bookable.
- A booking form that earns its fields. Three questions, and what each one is worth on the call itself.
- Buffers and daily limits that stop a full pipeline from eating the preparation time around it.
- Reminder sequences for calls booked more than a week out, which is where most of the missed ones sit.
- Booking the second meeting from inside the first, so the follow-up never returns to email.
- A one-page checklist to hand a new rep in their first week, and a short audit to run each quarter.

## Who it's for

- Account executives who own their own calendar and their own number.
- Sales managers who want every rep's page set up the same way without policing it.
- Marketing and RevOps teams responsible for what happens after the form is submitted.
