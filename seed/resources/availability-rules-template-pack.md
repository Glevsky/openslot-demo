Availability is five settings: working hours per weekday, buffers before and after, a daily limit, minimum notice, and the date range you are bookable in. Most people set the hours, skip the other four, and then wonder why the week has no shape to it.

This pack is nine setups that teams already run, written out as plain numbers you can copy into a booking page in a couple of minutes. Nothing in it is clever. The combination is what matters, and the combination is the part most calendars are missing.

It is free and there is no form. Take the one closest to your week, change the numbers until it is yours, and keep the daily limit even when it looks generous on a quiet Monday.

## What's inside

- Focus mornings. Nothing bookable before 1pm, four meetings a day, 12 hours minimum notice, so the morning stays whole.
- Two meeting days. Bookings on Tuesday and Thursday only, 15 minute buffers, no daily cap, for work that needs long stretches.
- The sales day. 9am to 5pm, 15 minutes after every call, eight calls a day, 4 hours minimum notice for late inbound.
- Clinic hours. 8am to 6pm with a closed hour at lunch, 10 minute buffers, 24 hours notice, and a daily limit per practitioner.
- Tutor evenings. 4pm to 9pm on weekdays and Saturday mornings, 2 hours notice, no cap, for people booking after school.
- Interview loops. 10am to 4pm, 15 minute buffers, three interviews a day per interviewer, so nobody spends a whole day on a panel.
- Support rotation. Bookable only during the week a person is on shift, six a day, and a 30 day range so the far future stays empty.
- Executive calendar. Two 45 minute windows a day, 48 hours minimum notice, bookable 10 days out and no further than that.
- Seasonal opening. A date range with an end date, for a program that runs for six weeks and should then stop taking bookings.

## Who it's for

- Operations leads setting booking pages up for a team for the first time, who want a starting point rather than an empty form.
- Anyone whose calendar is technically correct and still unusable by Wednesday afternoon.
- Teams moving from one shared page to a page per person, where everyone should start from the same rules.
