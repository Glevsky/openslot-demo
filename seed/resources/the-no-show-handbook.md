A no-show is the most expensive thing that can happen to a booking. The slot was held, the preparation was done, and nothing came back from it. This handbook is what we learned after studying 1.2 million bookings made through Openslot, turned into steps you can apply this week. It is written for the person who owns the number rather than the person who reports it.

Two numbers hold the book together. Without reminders, 19 percent of invitees did not show up. With an email reminder, an SMS reminder and a reschedule link inside the message, 11 percent did not. The chapters explain where that distance comes from and which part of it you can take for yourself.

It is deliberately short on theory. Each chapter names a setting, says what to change it to, and says what to expect. Where the evidence is thin, the page says so rather than dressing a hunch up as a finding. Every claim carries the count of bookings behind it, so you can decide how much weight it deserves.

## What's inside

- The study in brief: how bookings were counted, what was excluded, and what the numbers do not prove.
- Reminder timing. The evening before and roughly an hour before did more than anything we tried on wording.
- The reschedule link, and why the missed meetings that go away mostly come back as moved ones.
- Lead time. Bookings made weeks ahead are missed more often, and a shorter date range is the fix.
- First meetings against repeat ones, and why the reminder matters most for people who have never met you.
- SMS: who it is worth it for, what it costs in attention, and how to write one that is not a nag.
- A worked example from a 14-clinic physiotherapy group that saw no-shows fall by 38 percent.
- What to do with the slot a reschedule frees up, so the same hour is not lost twice.
- A one-page setup checklist, plus the weekly number to watch afterward.

## Who it's for

- Clinic and practice managers whose calendar is full while the rooms sit empty.
- Operations leads at services businesses where a missed appointment is a lost hour.
- Anyone running a booking page who has never changed the default reminder settings.
