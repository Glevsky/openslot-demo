## Plans and billing

- Free. $0. One booking page, one calendar, email reminders, Openslot branding.
- Pro. $12 per user per month billed yearly, or $15 per user per month billed monthly.
- Team. $20 per user per month billed yearly, or $24 per user per month billed monthly.
- Enterprise. Custom pricing.

Billing yearly saves 20 percent against the monthly price. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax, which is added where it applies. Paid plans are charged per user. Users added during a term are charged from the day they are added. Users removed come off the bill at the next renewal.

Paid plans renew automatically, monthly or yearly, until they are cancelled. If a payment fails we retry it and tell you. If it keeps failing the workspace moves to the Free plan, which keeps your data and turns off paid features.

## The trial
