## Who we are

Openslot is made by Openslot Technologies, Lda., Rua do Relógio 14, 1200-001 Lisboa, Portugal. For the data described here we are the controller, which means we decide why it is collected and what happens to it. This policy was last updated on 1 February 2026.

## Who it covers

Two kinds of people use Openslot. Account holders sign up, build booking pages and connect a calendar. Invitees never sign up; they open a booking page and pick a time.

For bookings made through a customer's own page, that customer decides what to ask an invitee and what to do with the answers. In those cases we handle the data on their instructions and they answer for it. We answer for the service itself.

## What we collect

From account holders:

- Account details: name, email address, a hashed password, time zone, and the workspace you belong to.
- Billing details on paid plans: company name, billing address, tax number where you have one, and the last four digits and expiry date of a card. We never see or store a full card number.
- What you create: booking pages, availability rules, form questions, message text, workflows and webhook settings.
- Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, and which pages were used inside the product.

From invitees:

- Name, email address, and the answers to whatever the booking page asks.
- The booking itself: event type, time, time zone, and whether it was later moved or cancelled.
- A phone number, when SMS reminders are switched on for that page and the invitee provides one.

## Why we use it

- To run the service: create bookings, place them on the right calendars, and send confirmations, reminders, reschedule and cancellation messages.
- To charge for paid plans and keep the accounting records we are required to keep.
- To answer support requests sent through the contact form.
- To keep the service secure and available, which includes logging, rate limiting and looking into abuse.
- To improve the product, using counts and aggregates wherever they will do.

We rely on the grounds available under applicable data protection law: performing our contract with an account holder, our legitimate interest in running and protecting the service, consent where consent is the right basis, and obligations we cannot avoid. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use booking content for advertising.

## Calendar access

When you connect a calendar we ask for the narrowest access the product can work with: reading when you are busy, and creating, updating and deleting the events Openslot made itself. We read the times of your other events to work out which slots are free. We do not store their titles, descriptions, attendees or notes. Disconnect a calendar in settings at any time and the access ends immediately.

## Who else handles it

A small number of companies help us run Openslot: hosting and databases, email delivery, SMS delivery, payment processing, error monitoring, and the tools support works in. Each one processes data on our instructions, only for the purpose we engaged it for, under written terms. The current list is available on request through the contact form.

## Where it is processed

Openslot runs from data centers in the European Union. Some of the companies above operate outside it. Where data leaves the region we use the transfer safeguards available under applicable data protection law. Enterprise workspaces can ask about data residency.

## How long we keep it

- Account data: while the account is open, then 90 days, then deleted.
- Bookings and invitee data: 24 months after the meeting, or sooner if the account holder deletes them. Deleting a booking page deletes the questions and answers attached to it.
- Billing records: as long as tax and accounting rules require, which is longer than the rest.
- Technical logs: 30 days. Security records kept longer are held apart from account content.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access inside the company is limited to the people whose work needs it, reviewed regularly and logged. Enterprise workspaces get an audit log of their own. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authority within the time applicable law allows.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to limit what we do with it, object to processing we base on our legitimate interest, and ask for your data in a portable form. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time, which does not affect what happened before. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

## How to ask

Send a request through the form at [contact](/contact) and say what you want. We answer within 30 days. If your booking was made through another company's page, we pass the request to them and tell you we have done it, because the data is theirs to act on.

## Changes

If we change this policy we change the date at the top. When a change affects what we collect or why, account holders are told inside the product before it takes effect.
