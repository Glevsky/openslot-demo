Some meetings do not fit a booking page. A booking page works when one side offers times and the other side picks. A project kickoff with six people, a board call, a panel interview with three interviewers and a candidate: nobody is the host, and every calendar has a say.

Meeting polls are for those. You propose a handful of times, the group votes, and you book the winner. They are live today on the Team plan.

## What shipped

A poll is a booking page with several candidate times and a vote instead of a pick.

You choose the duration, then add times. Openslot suggests them from your own availability and flags any that clash with the calendar of a teammate you add, so you do not propose a time your own people cannot make. You can add up to 20.

Each voter gets a link. They see the times in their own time zone, mark each one as yes, if needed, or no, and leave a name. No account is required. As votes come in, you see a grid of people against times, with the best times sorted to the top. Votes update as they arrive, so you can watch a time pull ahead without refreshing.

When you are ready, choose Book on a time. Openslot creates the booking, puts it in the calendar of every teammate on the poll, sends the invitation to every voter with the reschedule and cancel links, and closes the poll so nobody votes on a meeting that already exists. Reminders, workflows and webhooks apply to the booking like any other.

If a proposed time gets taken in your calendar while the poll is open, it is marked as no longer available, for the remaining voters and for you.

## Who it is for

Teams that run meetings with more than one person on their own side: project managers starting an engagement, executive assistants scheduling a board, recruiters arranging panel interviews, agencies setting a kickoff with a client's team. Anyone who has ever sent "does Tuesday at 2 or Thursday at 10 work for everyone" and then waited.

Polls also suit meetings with people outside your company, because voters need nothing but the link. It replaces the spreadsheet, or the thread of replies, that usually stands in for one.

## How to turn it on

Polls are on for every Team workspace. To create one:

1. From the dashboard, choose New, then Meeting poll.
2. Set the duration and give the poll a name.
3. Add times. Use the suggestions from your availability or pick them from the grid.
4. Add the teammates whose calendars should be checked and who should receive the booking.
5. Share the link with the voters. They see every time in their own zone, so there is nothing to set for them.
6. When the votes are in, choose Book on the time you want.

Workspace admins who do not want members creating polls can turn them off under Settings, then Admin controls, then Features. Polls a member has already created stay open until they are booked or closed.
