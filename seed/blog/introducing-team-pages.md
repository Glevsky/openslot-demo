Team pages are live today. A Team page is a booking page with more than one person behind it, and it comes in the two forms we described [last month](/blog/round-robin-vs-collective): round-robin, where each booking goes to one member of the group in rotation, and collective, where every member has to be free and the meeting lands on all of their calendars.

## What shipped

- Round-robin pages, with three rotation rules: strict, availability-first, and weighted. You pick one per page.
- Collective pages, with their own working hours so the group can protect a shared window rather than rely on overlap.
- Combined availability that reads every member's connected calendar, so a page never offers a time someone has already given away.
- Reassignment on cancel. If the assigned member cancels a round-robin booking, the invitee keeps the time and the booking goes to the next person in rotation who is free then.
- Members can be on any number of Team pages at once, with the same connected calendar behind all of them.

Everything an ordinary booking page has, a Team page has too: buffers, daily limits, minimum notice, date ranges, email reminders with reschedule and cancel links, and time-zone detection for the invitee.

## Who it is for

Any group where the invitee needs a role rather than a person, or a specific set of people rather than one. Sales teams sharing a single intro-call link. Recruiting teams running screens and panels. Support teams taking sessions on rotation. Onboarding calls where an account manager and an implementation lead both need to be present.

Orbital Recruiting, a 60-recruiter agency in Berlin that places engineers, ran the beta on their interview scheduling. Before, the time from a candidate saying yes to their first interview averaged 6 days, most of it spent finding a recruiter with a free hour. On a round-robin page it is now 1.5 days, at 2,400 interviews a month.

> We stopped being the bottleneck. A candidate picks a time, a recruiter gets the interview, and nobody in between has to send an email.
>
> Felix Brandt, Head of Talent, Orbital Recruiting

## Which plan

Team pages are part of the Team plan, at $20 per user per month billed yearly or $24 monthly, and are included in Enterprise. If you are on Free or Pro, you can start a 14-day trial of Team from the [pricing page](/pricing) without adding a card. Every member who takes bookings on a Team page needs a seat.

## How to turn it on

1. Get your teammates into the workspace. Go to Settings, then Members, then Invite, and send an invitation to everyone who will take bookings. Each person connects their own calendar under Settings, then Calendars, after accepting.
2. Go to Booking pages, then New page, and choose Team page.
3. Pick Round-robin or Collective. For round-robin, choose the rotation rule. For collective, set the shared working hours.
4. Add members, name the event, set the duration and location, and save.

The page gets a link like any other. Share it the way you share any booking link, or send it from your CRM. A booking shows up on the assigned member's calendar within a few seconds of the invitee confirming.

## What is next

Two things we heard most often from the beta: a way to let an invitee pick a specific member when they want to, and a rule that keeps a returning invitee with the member they met last time.
