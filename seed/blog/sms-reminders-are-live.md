SMS reminders are out of beta today and available on every paid plan. If your invitees are the kind of people who do not see an email until the meeting is over, this is the release for you.

We have been running SMS with a group of beta workspaces since the start of the year. Last month we published [what we learned](/blog/what-1-2-million-bookings-taught-us-about-no-shows) from 1.2 million bookings: 19 percent of invitees did not show when there were no reminders, and 11 percent did not when there was email, SMS and a reschedule link in the message. That result is why this shipped the way it did.

## What shipped

- SMS as a reminder step. Add it to any booking page alongside email, with its own timing. Most beta workspaces settled on two: the evening before, and one hour before.
- Reschedule and cancel links in every message. The invitee can move or cancel the booking from the text without opening anything else, and the host's calendar updates as it would from any other reschedule.
- The invitee's own time. Every message shows the meeting time in the zone the invitee booked in, not the host's.
- International numbers. Invitees enter a number with a country code on the booking form, and the message goes where they are.
- A phone number question for the booking form, with a consent line that says what the number will be used for, and a setting to make it required or optional.

Messages are short by design: the meeting name, the time, the two links. There is no template to edit and nothing to design.

## Who it is for

Anyone whose invitees are away from their inbox when the meeting is close. Clinics whose patients are at work. Tutoring services whose students are teenagers. Recruiters whose candidates are in another job all day. Field sales teams whose customers are on site.

Halcyon Health, 14 physiotherapy clinics across Manchester and the north of England, was in the beta from the first week. With SMS reminders and reschedule links on every appointment page, their no-shows fell by 38 percent, and the rollout across all 14 clinics took 2 days.

> The text is what patients actually read. The reschedule link is what turns a missed appointment into a moved one.
>
> Dr. Amara Whitfield, Operations Director, Halcyon Health

## Which plan

SMS reminders are included in Pro, at $12 per user per month billed yearly or $15 monthly, and in Team and Enterprise. The Free plan keeps email reminders, which remain the floor for every booking page. Plan details are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## How to turn it on

1. Go to Settings, then Reminders, then Add SMS. Choose when the message goes out. You can add more than one step.
2. Open the booking page the reminders should apply to, then Questions, and add the Phone number question. Mark it required if a reminder by text matters for that kind of meeting, or leave it optional and let invitees decide.
3. Save. Invitees who give a number get email and SMS. Invitees who do not still get email.

Existing bookings are not affected. Reminders are attached when a booking is made, so the first texts go out for bookings created after you turn SMS on.

## What is next

The most common beta request was a way for an invitee to reply to the message rather than tap a link. We are looking at it carefully, because a reply that means move it and a reply that means thanks look alike to software. If there is a reminder behavior you need that is not here, the [contact form](/contact) reaches the product team directly.
